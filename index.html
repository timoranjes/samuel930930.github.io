About

I received my Ph.D. from Johns Hopkins University in 2021, under the supervision of Prof. Rama Chellappa. I obtained my B.Sc. and M.Phil. from CUHK, and M.S. from the University of Maryland. I was a postdoctoral fellow at MINDS, JHU.
